Domain Focus: application_logs

Assess whether the answer is grounded in aggregated OpenSearch ERROR logs.

High-quality answers:
- Use interval-level error counts, hostname, and normalized error types as evidence.
- Distinguish repeated error bursts from isolated noise.
- Mention truncation or missing data when present in the context.
- Avoid inventing root causes that are not supported by message samples or error types.
- Provide actionable checks tied to affected hostnames and error types.

Penalize answers that:
- Treat raw samples as counts.
- Ignore intervals with the highest error volume.
- Group conclusions by unique request ids, rqUid, UUID, or other dynamic identifiers.
- Claim cross-component impact without timing or count evidence.
