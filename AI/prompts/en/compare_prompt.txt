TASK: explain how run A differs from run B and why. A is the assessed run (usually the newer one), B is the baseline it is compared with.

INPUT DATA (JSON below):
- facts — comparison facts computed by the program: runs A and B (verdicts, key RPS, test type, window), the outcome of the comparison and its reasons, comparability caveats, verdicts by domain, metric changes by p95. A metric change is A relative to B in percent; the assessment "better" or "worse" already accounts for the meaning of the metric. Do not recompute them.
- findings — findings of the report analyses of both runs by domain. Identifiers look like "A:jvm:f1" (a finding of run A) and "B:database:f2" (a finding of run B).
- system — the system description: components, dependencies, known constraints. May be empty.

RULES:
1. Use only the data from the JSON. Do not invent services, metrics, causes or numbers.
2. The outcome of the comparison (better, worse, no change, mixed, not comparable) is computed by the program: do not change it in headline and summary. If the data contradicts it, say so in caveats.
3. Base the causes of the changes on findings and list their identifiers in evidence. A finding that only one run has is the main candidate for a cause.
4. Write numbers as in the data, without spaces inside a number (1320, not 1 320), with the same units. Take the values of A and B in key_changes from the data.
5. Separate fact from hypothesis: phrase a hypothesis with "likely" or "probably".
6. Reflect every comparability caveat of facts in caveats; if data is missing, say exactly what.
7. All texts in English, short and to the point: the answer is read by a manager who has one minute.
8. The texts are read by a person, not by a program: do not write JSON field names (change_pct, top_changes, facts, findings and the like) — name the quantities in words. Write load as RPS.
9. Give finding identifiers only in evidence[].finding_id; do not write them in other fields.
10. Respect the limits: headline — one phrase of up to 120 characters; summary — 1–2 sentences; key_changes — 2 to 5 changes; causes, caveats and next_checks — up to 3 items, each up to 140 characters, next_checks in descending order of importance.

ANSWER — strict JSON without text around it:
{
  "headline": "the main difference of A from B in one phrase",
  "summary": "1–2 sentences: how A changed relative to B and why",
  "key_changes": [{"label": "what and where, up to 60 characters, for example 'response p95, load tool'", "value_a": "value of A with units, up to 20 characters", "value_b": "value of B with units, up to 20 characters", "assessment": "better | worse | same"}],
  "causes": ["a likely cause of the change"],
  "evidence": [{"finding_id": "A:jvm:f1", "role": "what this finding confirms"}],
  "caveats": ["what makes the comparison approximate or what the outcome does not cover"],
  "next_checks": ["what to check before the next test"],
  "confidence": "high | medium | low"
}
