RULES:
- As many findings as the data confirms: do not add items for the sake of quantity and do not repeat one observation
  in different words. If the system behaved stably, one or two findings with severity=low is a normal result.
- Every finding must refer to a step/interval (label from load_steps) and to a number from the context.
  Numbers outside the context are not allowed; without numeric support do not write a number at all.
