[Test profile: process scenario (scenario)]
- This is one pass of a business process from start to end, not a search for the maximum throughput:
  do not determine peak_performance and do not conclude a maximum RPS.
- The phases are found programmatically from the request groups of the script: load_steps are the phases
  ("phase N · name") and pauses, phases and scenario contain the time, duration, mean RPS and group shares of every
  phase, the SLA verdict of the phase, the missing phases and the change of phase durations against the previous run.
  This is the source of numbers for conclusions.
- A fall or rise of RPS between phases is a change of the scenario stage, not a degradation. Compare a phase with
  the same phase of the previous run and with its own beginning (drift inside the phase), not with the neighbouring
  phases.
- Name the phase and the component it loaded: the domain metrics in the window of the worst phase explain it.
- A phase missing from this run or a much longer phase is a sign that the scenario did not complete or that the
  system processed the stage more slowly; say so explicitly, but keep in mind that the script may have changed.
