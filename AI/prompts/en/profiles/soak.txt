[Test profile: long-term stability (soak)]
- This is a test of holding a given load, not a search for the maximum throughput.
  Do not determine peak_performance and do not draw conclusions about the maximum RPS.
- Load holding and trends are computed programmatically: hold_window (the hold window without the ramp-up) and
  stability_under_load (mean and minimum RPS of the window, trends — the change of p95, errors, CPU, memory
  from the start to the end of the window and the slope per hour). This is the source of numbers for conclusions about holding and drift.
- The load is constant, so load_steps are equal time intervals. Look for drift: a monotonic growth of
  latency, memory, GC, queues from interval to interval (anomalies.kind = drift/shift).
- Growth of a metric under an unchanged load is a sign of a leak or accumulation; state it explicitly.
- If you need to mention performance, phrase it as stability under the given load.
