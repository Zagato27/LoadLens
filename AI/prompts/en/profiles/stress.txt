[Test profile: stress]
- Behaviour beyond the design capacity. Look for the saturation point: the step after which the throughput
  stops growing while latency and errors grow. The saturation point is the last stable
  step, not a short peak; the value is already computed in designated_peak_performance and saturation.
- Name the limiting resource (saturation.limiting_resource, if found) and the kind of degradation
  (saturation.failure_mode): growing latency, errors or falling throughput.
- When there is a target step (deterministic_sla.target_window), the verdict is decided on it; the saturation
  point is a separate result: overload above the target step is the expected outcome of a stress test, not a reason to lower the verdict.
