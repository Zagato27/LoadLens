Domain Focus: lt_framework

Evaluation Criteria Addendum
- Look for RPS/throughput, error rate, latency percentiles, saturation point, peak_performance for capacity/step tests, drop point, signs of an unstable test environment.
- For stability/soak tests do not require peak_performance: assess the load hold and the absence of cumulative degradation.
- For spike tests do not require peak_performance: assess the growth of p95 and errors in the spike window relative to the background and the return to the background after it.
- A good candidate does not confuse peak throughput with a short spike and is careful when speaking about stable performance.
- Recommendations must be useful for the load scenario: profile, pacing, generators, target RPS, think time, checks.

Evaluation Steps
1. Check RPS, latency p95/p99, errors and the possible saturation point.
2. Check whether the candidate describes peak_performance and the moment of degradation correctly, and only where this applies.
3. Check whether problems of the load testing tool are separated from problems of the system under test.
4. Check whether the recommendations lead to a better scenario and a better interpretation of the test results.
