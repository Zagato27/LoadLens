Domain Focus: microservices

Evaluation Criteria Addendum
- Look for latency growth, error rate, saturation by service, degraded dependencies, uneven load distribution, cascading failures.
- A good candidate singles out specific services/endpoints and distinguishes the source of the degradation from its consequences.
- Recommendations must target specific components: service, deployment, pod, instance, endpoint.

Evaluation Steps
1. Check latency, error rate, throughput and resilience by service.
2. Check whether the most degraded services and the critical call chains are identified.
3. Check for specifics: endpoint, service name, pod, time window, threshold.
4. Check whether the recommendations help to localize and remove the degradation in the service chain.
