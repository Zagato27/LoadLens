Domain Focus: JVM

Evaluation Criteria Addendum
- Look for heap/metaspace pressure, GC pause, GC frequency, thread saturation, CPU pressure of JVM processes.
- Note signs of a memory leak, stop-the-world pauses, degradation after the load grows.
- A good candidate links JVM symptoms to a specific service, pod/job or time window.

Evaluation Steps
1. Check JVM memory: heap, non-heap, metaspace, old/new generation.
2. Check GC: pauses, frequency, anomalous growth, impact on latency/throughput.
3. Check threads, CPU and locks if they are present in the context.
4. Check whether specific actions for the JVM or the service are proposed rather than generic phrases.
