Domain Focus: database

Evaluation Criteria Addendum
- Look for query latency, locks, slow queries, connection saturation, queueing, replication lag, errors/timeouts.
- A good candidate links DB degradation to a specific metric, table, instance or time interval.
- Recommendations must be tied to SQL/indexes/connection pool/DB resources rather than be abstract.

Evaluation Steps
1. Check latency, throughput and errors on the DB side.
2. Check for signs of saturation: connections, locks, buffers, replication, disk IO.
3. Check whether root causes are identified and distinguished from secondary symptoms.
4. Check whether the recommendations give operationally useful steps for the DBA or the service team.
