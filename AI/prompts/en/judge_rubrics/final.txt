Domain Focus: final

Evaluation Criteria Addendum
- Look for a synthesis across domains: the final verdict, the main bottlenecks, cross-domain links, SLA risks, peak_performance only for step and stress tests.
- For stability/soak tests assess stability under load: RPS hold (stability_under_load.hold), latency/errors/checks, resource trends (stability_under_load.trends) and accumulating degradation, without requiring peak_performance.
- For spike tests assess the reaction to the spike: growth of latency and errors in the spike window relative to the background (overshoot), recovery time, RPS dips (spike_analysis); peak_performance is not required.
- For stress tests assess the saturation point, the nature of the degradation and the limiting resource (saturation).
- A good candidate does not just repeat the domain blocks but sets priorities and explains the outcome of the test.
- Recommendations must be ranked by their impact on the test result and tied to specific teams/components.

Evaluation Steps
1. Check that the final conclusion is consistent with the domain candidates and the overall context.
2. Check whether 1-3 main factors of success or degradation are identified.
3. Check whether SLA, errors, latency and peak_performance are taken into account where they apply.
4. Check whether the final text helps to make an engineering decision based on the test results.
