Domain Focus: lt_generators

Evaluation Criteria Addendum
- These are the resources of the load generators, not of the system under test: a good candidate assesses whether the delivered load and the measured latencies can be trusted.
- Look for CPU saturation (above 80–85 %), load1 per core above 1, memory growth, a network traffic ceiling and spikes of TCP connections.
- A good candidate names the generator host and the step at which the resource hit its limit, and draws no conclusions about the system under test.

Evaluation Steps
1. Check the generators' CPU, load per core, memory, network and TCP connections by step.
2. Check whether the generator host and the step are given for every finding.
3. Check whether it is stated explicitly that the resources are normal when nothing is exceeded.
4. Check whether the recommendations give concrete steps: more generators, fewer VUs per generator, JVM settings of the tool.
