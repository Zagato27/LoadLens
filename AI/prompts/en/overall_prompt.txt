- In the JSON context: load_steps, timeline (metric × step across all domains), baseline, deterministic_sla,
  designated_peak_performance, test_profile, domain packs and test type data (stability_under_load for soak,
  spike_analysis for spike, saturation for stress).

ANALYSIS FOCUS:
1. Step where degradation began: from timeline determine at which step latency/errors first grew in lt_framework and
   which metrics of other domains changed at the same step (JVM, DB, Kafka, resources, logs). A match by step is
   a correlation; phrase it as a coincidence in time, not as a proven cause.
2. Chains between components: describe the sequence "load → resource/component → user-facing metric"
   only where every link is confirmed by numbers from timeline or the domain reports.
3. SLA: deterministic_sla is the source of truth. The final verdict must not contradict deterministic_sla.verdict;
   if target_rps_check.passed=true, do not write that the target RPS was not reached, and vice versa. Do not recompute the SLA yourself.
4. Result by test type (test_profile.normalized_test_type):
   - step/stress — maximum performance only from designated_peak_performance (stable_max, method
     last_stable_step); for stress call it the saturation point and describe saturation.failure_mode and
     saturation.limiting_resource. Do not take RPS from the microservices domain and do not use short peaks.
   - soak — load hold and trends from stability_under_load (hold, trends, flagged_trends) instead of
     peak_performance.
   - spike — spike level, p95 and error growth relative to the background, and recovery time from spike_analysis.
5. Baseline: if it is available — name the key differences from the previous run (key_deltas) and take them into account
   in the verdict (a regression against a run with verdict "pass" is a risk). If the baseline is unavailable — do not mention a comparison with the past.
6. Do not use domain findings with verification.status="unverified" as grounds for the verdict — mention them
   only with the note "not confirmed by data".

REQUIREMENTS:
- Final findings are systemic (cross-component) or the most significant domain findings; do not retell all domain findings.
- In every finding: the step by label, numbers from timeline/domain reports, the affected components.
- Prioritize recommendations by systemic impact and link them to findings via for_finding_ids.
- verdict_rationale is mandatory and must explain exactly the final status (consistent with deterministic_sla.verdict).
- Fill in the peak_performance field from designated_peak_performance; if stable_max is absent or
  designated_peak_performance.not_applicable=true — do not add it.
