TASK: verification pass over the final load test report.

In the input JSON:
- analysis — the final report (verdict, verdict_rationale, findings[], recommended_actions[]). Every finding has
  verification.status: verified — the numbers are confirmed by the collected metrics; qualitative — there are no numbers;
  unverified — the numbers in the finding were NOT found in the data (listed in unverified_numbers).
- unverified_findings — the findings with status unverified and their unconfirmed numbers.
- context — compact test data: load_steps, timeline (metric × step), deterministic_sla,
  designated_peak_performance, baseline.

WHAT TO DO:
1. Revise verdict and verdict_rationale relying ONLY on the findings with status verified and qualitative and on
   deterministic_sla. Unverified findings cannot be grounds for the verdict.
2. If, after excluding the unverified findings, no grounds for "fail"/"risk" remain and deterministic_sla does not
   contradict this, soften the verdict. If the grounds are no longer sufficient for any conclusion — "no_data".
   The verdict must not contradict deterministic_sla.verdict; whether target_rps was reached comes only from
   deterministic_sla.target_rps_check.
3. Do NOT delete unverified findings: keep them in findings without changing the id/summary/evidence_* fields, but append
   the note " (not confirmed by data)" to the end of summary if it is not there yet. Do not add new numbers to such findings.
4. Carry over the other findings, recommendations and peak_performance unchanged. Do not add new findings or numbers
   that are not in context.
5. verdict_rationale is mandatory: rewrite it so that it names the chosen status (start with Status "<label>": ...), only
   confirmed facts (with numbers from context) and deterministic_sla. An empty rationale is not allowed; if needed, add one
   phrase about how many findings are not confirmed by data.

RESPONSE FORMAT: STRICTLY one JSON object of the same schema as analysis (verdict, verdict_rationale, confidence, findings[],
recommended_actions[], affected_components[], peak_performance?). verdict is a code: "pass" — passed, "risk" — risks found,
"fail" — failed, "no_data" — insufficient data; <label> is Passed, Risks found, Failed or Insufficient data respectively.
All text fields in English. No text outside JSON.
