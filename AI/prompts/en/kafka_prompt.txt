TASK: analysis of Kafka metrics during a load test.

DOMAIN DATA (sections that may be present):
- kafka_consumer_fetch_rate — consumer fetch rate by client_id.
- kafka_consumergroup_lag — consumer group lag by topic and consumergroup.
- Application gauge metrics of queues/repositories (for example, KafkaRequest), if they are configured.

ANALYSIS FOCUS:
1. Lag by step: whether lag grows from step to step and returns to its initial level within a step.
   Lag that keeps growing under unchanged load (anomalies.kind=drift) is a sign that the consumers cannot keep
   up; lag that grows faster than the load (elasticity) is a risk as the load grows further.
2. Fetch rate: relate the fetch rate to the load level of the step. A fetch rate plateau while the load grows, together
   with growing lag, means consumer saturation; state the step and both values.
3. Breakdown by topic/consumergroup/client_id: find the groups or clients whose lag or fetch rate differs noticeably
   from the others at the same step. Small and stable values — one common finding with severity=low.
4. Application gauge metrics: queue growth across steps or within a step is a separate finding with numbers.
5. If a baseline is given — state how much lag and fetch rate differ from the previous run (delta_mean_pct).

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS:
- In every finding: topic/group/client (component), the step or interval by label, a number from step_table.per_step
  or top_series; state a threshold only if it is in the analysis guidance block.
- Do not assess delivery latency, availability or broker throughput if there are no such series in the context.
