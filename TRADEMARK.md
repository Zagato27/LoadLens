## What you may do

- Use the name to refer to the project: "LoadLens", "the LoadLens project", "compatible with
  LoadLens".
- Say that your product or service is "based on LoadLens", "built on LoadLens" or "a fork of
  LoadLens", as long as it is clear that it is not the official project.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy of a release (source or Docker image) that you distribute
  or run, including inside your company.
- Use screenshots of LoadLens in articles, talks and reviews.

## What you may not do

- Distribute a modified version under the name LoadLens or a confusingly similar name
  (for example "LoadLens Pro", "LoadLensX", "loadlens-enterprise"): a fork that changes the
  product must use its own name and logo. Keeping the `loadlens_app` package name or internal
  identifiers is fine; what matters is the name shown to users.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation with, the
  LoadLens project or its author.
- Register the name, the logo or a confusingly similar mark as a trademark, domain name,
  company name or package name of your own.
